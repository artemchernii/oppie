# Business Idea Session — research context

This is a compact record of what the prior session established. It is context for research, not a set of verified current market facts. Re-check claims before spending money.

- €20k available capital

- willing to go full-time after profitability is demonstrated
- high risk tolerance

- global search, with a particular interest in Europe and local adaptation

## What we learned about the problem

The research process itself became unusable when it produced long, optimistic essays. oppie.lab exists to turn research into compact, comparable decision objects.

The product should help answer:

> What deserves the next test, and what should we stop spending time on?

## The more interesting thesis

Instead of starting with “what startup should we invent?”, oppie.lab can search for:

> Proven business models that have not saturated every market.

The research path is:

```text
YC / company / acquisition signal
  → understand the actual business mechanism
  → identify who pays and why
  → check competitors in Europe / UK / Portugal / another target market
  → check Reddit and community pain
  → compare pricing and operational differences
  → identify a local wedge
  → run a cheap validation test
```

## Sources to use repeatedly
