## Why

oppie.lab is meant to discover opportunities, not merely organise problems that a user already
knows. A person should be able to provide a market, geography, role, industry or loose prompt such
as “financial operations in European RIAs”, and receive a small set of source-backed problems and
business patterns worth investigating.

The current product direction starts too late. It assumes a person has already framed the problem
and asks them to maintain a research record. That is the opposite of the core value.

## What changes

- Define discovery as the primary product loop.
- Accept sparse user input: market, geography, industry, role, constraint or free-form prompt.
- Search source classes that reveal real work, money and buyers.
- Extract recurring workflows and cluster sources into candidate problems.
- Identify existing businesses, vendors, contractors and pricing around each problem.
- Present “copy”, “adapt”, and “start from scratch” paths as hypotheses, not conclusions.
- Require human acceptance before a discovered problem or business pattern becomes a tracked record.
- Keep every candidate traceable to quotes, figures, URLs, dates and source status.
- Make the existing rubric a later inspection tool for accepted candidates, not the discovery engine.

## Core question

> Given a direction, what painful work already happens here, who pays for it, and what business
> already serves it well enough to copy, adapt or challenge?

## Not in this change

- No promise that the engine can prove a business will succeed.
- No automatic application of candidates, verdicts, scores or business ideas.
- No hidden market-size estimate or probability of success.
- No scraping of private or restricted sources.
- No teams, billing, notifications or scheduled crawling.
- No final decision about whether a discovered business can legally be copied; intellectual property,
  regulation and ethics remain explicit research questions.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `problem-discovery` — turning sparse direction into source-backed candidate problems and business
  patterns that a person can accept, reject or investigate.

### Modified Capabilities

- `problem-analysis` — analysis begins after a candidate is accepted; it does not pretend to be the
  discovery engine.
