# Pricing and unit economics: white-label MTD for Income Tax / bookkeeping back office for UK practices, staffed in Lisbon vs India / Philippines / South Africa

Research date: 2026-10-04. All prices quoted in original currency. FX is NOT sourced here; where a conversion is shown it uses an explicitly labelled assumption (EUR 1 = GBP 0.85; USD 1 = GBP 0.75) that the report writer should replace with a dated rate.

Source-quality note: most retail-fee figures come from firm marketing blogs and aggregator guides, not from a formal fee survey. I found no ICAEW, AccountingWEB or Practice Index MTD fee survey with published numbers. Treat retail ranges as "advertised market ranges", not measured averages.

## Retail: what UK practices charge landlords and sole traders for MTD quarterly updates and the Final Declaration

### Takeaway
Advertised retail is roughly £40–£125 per quarterly update plus £150–£300 for the Final Declaration (about £450–£800 a year), or a bundled £15–£60/month MTD add-on / £30–£120/month all-in package. A plain self-assessment return without MTD sells for about £150–£500.

### Cited Findings
- AccountingFirms.co.uk (article dated 4 June 2026): one-off MTD setup and onboarding "£100–£300"; MTD for Income Tax (sole trader/landlord) "£15–£60 per month"; quarterly MTD submissions "£40–£120 per quarter"; full bundled MTD package "£30–£120 per month"; MTD-compatible software "£10–£35 per month". It cites no survey. Quote: "Most charge £15–£60 per month for MTD for Income Tax, or bundle it into a £30–£120 monthly package." — [AccountingFirms.co.uk](https://www.accountingfirms.co.uk/blog/making-tax-digital-accountant-fees/)
- Landlord-focused guides put the quarterly update at £75–£125 per quarter and the Final Declaration at £150–£300, giving £450–£800 a year; standalone quarterly support "£100 to £250 per quarter" (search-snippet summary across these pages, not individually fetched) — [Latch](https://www.uselatch.co.uk/blog/making-tax-digital-cost-landlords-2026); [MTD.digital](https://mtd.digital/mtd-income-tax/mtd-cost/); [UK Property Accountants](https://www.ukpropertyaccountants.co.uk/accountant-for-mtd-roles-cost-explained/); [Daykin Scott](https://www.daykinscott.co.uk/mtd-itsa-cost-uk/)
- Pre-MTD baseline: sole-trader self-assessment returns are advertised at roughly £150–£500, most commonly £200–£400 (+VAT) (search-snippet summary of firm guides) — [Sleek](https://sleek.com/uk/resources/how-much-do-accountants-charge-for-self-assessment/); [DG Accountancy](https://dgaccountancy.co.uk/resources/sole-trader-accountant-cost/); [Eternity Accountants](https://eternityaccountants.co.uk/accountants-fees/)
- Initor Global (an outsourcer) published guidance encouraging practices to price MTD IT on top of outsourcing costs — [Initor Global: use outsourcing to price MTD](https://initor-global.co.uk/use-outsourcing-to-price-mtd-for-income-tax-services-effectively/) (not fetched in full)

### Inferences
- Re-pricing pattern: practices are moving from a single annual SA fee (~£200–£400) to a monthly subscription (£15–£60 extra, or £30–£120 bundled), i.e. annual retail of roughly £360–£1,440 for a bundled client. Midpoint of the quarterly+FD model (£100 × 4 + £225) ≈ £625/year.
- Retail-to-wholesale gap: against Initor's "Full" wholesale price of £240–£420/year (below), a practice charging £450–£800/year keeps roughly £30–£560 per client. This is the margin the white-label must fit inside.

### Gaps
- No formal fee survey (ICAEW, AccountingWEB, Practice Index, Bright/IRIS) with MTD-specific medians found. Retail ranges are vendor-marketing figures.
- No data on what share of practices bundle vs charge per quarter.

## Wholesale: what UK-facing outsourcers charge practices

### Takeaway
Verified: Initor Global's fixed MTD IT price is £10–£35 per client per month (June 2025). Indian pay-as-you-go rates of £10–£13/hour are verified (Doshi). The brief's "QX dedicated FTE £1,000–£1,800/month" is NOT what QX now publishes: QX's August 2026 page says £1,500–£2,000/month for an accountant/senior accountant and £2,500–£3,000 for a manager.

### Cited Findings
- Initor Global fixed MTD IT pricing (published 26 June 2025), per business per month — [Initor Global](https://initor-global.co.uk/fixed-price-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-services-from-initor-global/):

  | Transactions/month | Lite | Premium | Full |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Up to 25 | £10 | £15 | £20 |
  | 26–50 | £15 | £20 | £25 |
  | 51–75 | £20 | £25 | £30 |
  | 76–100 | £25 | £30 | £35 |

  Lite = "Monthly data collection and bookkeeping – any software" with monthly bank reconciliation; Premium adds "quarterly summary of income and expenses submissions" and "file quarterly return via customer software"; Full adds "prepare income and expenses account", "file final declaration", "prepare final self-assessment tax return, simple in scope". "Simple self-assessment means MTD IT income and expenses plus any dividends or PAYE." A separate engagement is required for each business a client operates. Initor also says its bookkeeper/accountant rates are "typically 50% less" than employing in the UK.
- QX Accounting Services (article dated 17 August 2026): FTE model "£1,500/month" to "£2,000/month" for accountant/senior accountant; "£2,500/month" to "£3,000/month" for a manager. Annual QX accountant £18,000–£24,000 vs in-house UK accountant base £35,000–£45,000 (total £54,000–£87,000 incl. recruitment, training, tech); claims "Up to 55-70% lower than in-house costs"; no hourly or per-job prices — [QX Accounting](https://qxaccounting.com/uk/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-outsource-accounting-services/). Contradicts the brief's £1,000–£1,800 (possibly an older QX figure; not found).
- Doshi Outsourcing (Gujarat, India) pay-as-you-go, ex-VAT: accounts and CT returns "£13 p/h (Min per job £100)"; self-assessment returns "£13 p/h (Min per job £20)"; bookkeeping "£10 p/h (Min per job £39)"; VAT returns "£10 p/h (Min per job £39)"; SA online filing "£5 p/s". No MTD-specific price — [Doshi Outsourcing](https://www.doshioutsourcing.com/pay-as-you-go-outsourced-accounting)
- Generic offshore guides: India bookkeepers "$10 and $25 per hour", "$1,200 and $2,000 per month per full-time equivalent"; Philippines "$10 to $25 per hour", "$1,800 to $2,500 per month" for dedicated staff (US-CPA-market aggregators, lower quality) — [Etisson](https://www.etisson.com/blog/outsourced-bookkeeping-rates); [Madras Accountancy](https://madrasaccountancy.com/blog-posts/outsourced-bookkeeping-cost-for-cpa-firms-2026-pricing-guide); [Helpware](https://helpware.com/blog/accounting-outsourcing-companies-philippines)

### Inferences
- Indian hourly wholesale (£10–£13/h) sets the price ceiling a Lisbon team competes against on cost; a Lisbon offer must win on time zone, EU data residency, language/culture, or quality, not on price per hour.
- Initor's "Full" tier (£20–£35/month = £240–£420/year) bundles 4 quarterly updates + Final Declaration + simple SA return — this is the clearest per-client wholesale benchmark for the product.

### Gaps
- Could not verify a QX figure of £1,000–£1,800/month.
- No per-client MTD price from Outbooks, Mindspace, Exuberant or QX found on fetched pages.
- No published gross margins for any accounting outsourcer found (QX, Outbooks, Initor are private; none publish margins).

## Time per task

### Takeaway
The only sourced estimate is IRIS: 1–2 hours per client per quarter "assuming the data arrives clean" (so 4–8 hours/year for quarterly updates alone). I found no sourced estimate for Final Declaration time or first-quarter catch-up time.

### Cited Findings
- IRIS (published 9 April 2026, updated 14 April 2026): "one to two hours per client per quarter, assuming the data arrives clean"; "If you have 50 MTD for IT clients, that means you will spend 200 to 400 hours a year"; "At 100 clients, you're looking at 800 hours"; "81% of accountants identified MTD as the single biggest operational challenge" (survey not named). IRIS sells MTD software, so it has an incentive to stress the workload — [IRIS](https://www.iris.co.uk/blog/accountancy/the-true-cost-of-mtd-and-how-the-latest-tech-can-help-you/)
- Search snippets from other blogs mention "45 to 90 minutes per quarter" with MTD software used well, and "what should take 30 minutes per client will instead take an hour" (not fetched; attribution uncertain among [MTD Making Tax Digital blog](https://mtd-makingtaxdigital.co.uk/blog/mtd-monthly-vs-quarterly/) and [AccountingWEB checklist](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/community/industry-insights/mtd-itsa-7-august-2026-deadline-first-quarterly-update-checklist-for))

### Inferences
- Bank-feed vs no-bank-feed split is unsourced; a plausible modelling band is 0.75h/quarter (clean bank feed) to 2h/quarter (spreadsheets/receipts) — label as assumption.

### Gaps
- No time-and-motion study or practice-management dataset found.
- Final Declaration and onboarding/catch-up hours: not found.

## Lisbon costs

### Takeaway
Lisbon accounting assistants/accountants earn about €1,240–€1,420 gross per month on salary sites (14 payments); an English-speaking role serving international clients was listed at €2K–€3K. Employer cost adds 23.75% TSU on all 14 payments, a tax-free meal allowance up to €10.46/day on card, and ~1% accident insurance — about 35–45% above gross.

### Cited Findings
- Indeed PT: "Contabilista" average €1,419/month in Lisbon (updated 20 Sep 2026, 22 reports); "Assistente contábil" €1,241/month in Lisbon (updated 15 May 2026, 12 reports) — small samples — [Indeed contabilista Lisboa](https://pt.indeed.com/career/salaries/Contabilista%20Certificado(a)%20-%20Lisboa/Lisboa); [Indeed assistente contábil Lisboa](https://pt.indeed.com/career/assistente-cont%C3%A1bil/salaries/Lisboa)
- Accounting assistant range €920 (2026 minimum wage) to €1,200, reaching €1,500–€1,700 with experience — [Jobted](https://www.jobted.pt/sal%C3%A1rio/assistente-contabilidade) / [FedFinance](https://www.fedfinance.pt/noticias-conselhos/quanto-ganha-um-contabilista-em-portugal) (search snippet)
- Glassdoor listing: an Accountant/Senior Accounting Assistant role in Lisbon at €2K–€3K (snippet; single ad) — [Glassdoor Lisbon accounting jobs](https://www.glassdoor.com/Job/lisbon-accounting-jobs-SRCH_IL.0,6_IC3192045_KO7,17.htm). Glassdoor showed ~380 accounting jobs in Lisbon (Feb 2026) and 562 in Portugal (Sep 2026).
- Senior Accountant Lisbon average "$30,400 per year" (Glassdoor snippet) — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/lisbon-portugal-senior-accountant-salary-SRCH_IL.0,15_IM1121_KO16,33.htm)
- 14 salary payments; employer TSU 23.75% applies to all 14; at least 22 working days' annual leave; ~1% mandatory work-accident insurance; total employer cost 35–45% above gross; 2026 minimum wage €920 ×14 = €12,880 — [Multiplier](https://www.usemultiplier.com/portugal/cost-of-employment); [Boundless](https://boundlesshq.com/blog/payroll-in-portugal/); [FedFinance labour code](https://www.fedfinance.pt/en/news/guide-to-the-portuguese-labor-code-navigating-employment-in-2026)
- Meal allowance 2026: exempt from IRS and Social Security up to €6.15/day in cash or €10.46/day on meal card (Portaria 51-B/2026/1, from 1 Jan 2026); above that, 11% employee + 23.75% employer — [Verestro](https://mealvouchers.verestro.com/pt/rules/portugal/); [despacho.pt](https://despacho.pt/guias/subsidio-alimentacao-2026)
- English-speaking accounting firms serving international clients exist in Lisbon (HK Consulting, Elevate, Contordem) — [HK Consulting](https://www.hkconsulting.pt/); [Elevate Accounting](https://www.elevateaccounting.pt/accountants-lisbon/)
- Portugal salaries "undercut Western European peers by 40-60%" (EOR marketing) — [Employsome](https://employsome.com/hire/portugal/average-salary-portugal/)

### Inferences (worked estimate, all assumptions labelled)
- Illustrative English-speaking accounts assistant at €1,600 gross/month (assumption, between Indeed averages and the €2K ad):
  - Salary €1,600 × 14 = €22,400
  - TSU 23.75% × €22,400 = €5,320
  - Meal card €10.46 × ~220 working days = ~€2,300 (exempt)
  - Accident insurance ~1% = ~€224
  - **Loaded ≈ €30,250/year** (≈ £25,700 at assumed 0.85)
- Productive hours (assumption): 40h × 52 = 2,080, minus 22 leave days and ~13 public holidays (unsourced; ~280h) = ~1,800h; at 75% utilisation ≈ 1,350 chargeable hours → **≈ €22/hour (≈ £19/h) direct labour**, before management, office/coworking, software, recruitment, and profit.
- At IRIS's 1–2h/quarter plus an assumed 2h Final Declaration, one client takes 6–10h/year → €134–€224 (≈ £114–£190) direct Lisbon labour vs Initor's £240–£420 "Full" price. Positive gross margin only at the upper Initor tiers or with efficient (≤1.5h/quarter) delivery.
- Compared with India at £10–£13/h (Doshi, a sale price including margin), Lisbon direct labour cost alone is ~1.5–2× the Indian sale price.

### Gaps
- Hays/Michael Page/Robert Walters Portugal 2026 guides not retrieved (PDF/gated); no shared-service-centre accounting salary bands sourced.
- No named Portuguese SSC serving UK accountancy practices found.
- Coworking/office cost in Lisbon: not researched (ran out of budget).
- Number of Portuguese public holidays and typical utilisation are assumptions.

## Offshore comparison: India, Philippines, South Africa

### Takeaway
India is cheapest (£10–£13/h PAYG; QX FTE £1,500–£2,000/month). Philippines dedicated FTE ~$1,800–$2,500/month through providers; local accountant salary ~₱30,000/month. South Africa bookkeeper rates R130–R250/h locally; no UK-practice-facing SA wholesale price found.

### Cited Findings
- India: see Doshi (£10–£13/h) and QX (£1,500–£2,000/month accountant FTE) above. "Outsourced teams in India typically cost around 35–45% of what UK in-house teams would cost" (snippet; provider marketing) — [Exuberant Global](https://exuberantglobal.co.uk/blog/outsourced-accounting-services-to-india)
- Philippines: accountant average salary ₱30,355/month (3,400 reports, Dec 2025) — [PayScale PH](https://www.payscale.com/research/PH/Job=Accountant/Salary); accounting/finance PHP 20,000–30,000 entry, 30,000–50,000 mid — [isuporta](https://isuporta.com/blog/philippines-outsourcing-cost-salary-guide-2026); provider FTE $1,800–$2,500/month — [Helpware](https://helpware.com/blog/accounting-outsourcing-companies-philippines)
- South Africa: accountant average R24,476/month; bookkeeper hourly R130–R180 entry, R180–R250 mid, R400+ experienced (snippets) — [Indeed ZA](https://za.indeed.com/career/accountant/salaries); [Okhantu](https://www.okhantu.co.za/guides/business-operations/financial-management/bookkeeper-cost-south-africa); [Ready Accounting](https://www.readyaccounting.co.za/blog/outsourced-bookkeeping-rates-cape-town-2026-guide)

### Inferences
- Philippines provider FTE ($1,800–$2,500 ≈ £1,350–£1,875 at assumed 0.75) is similar to QX India FTE pricing, and a Lisbon loaded employee (~£2,140/month on the illustration above) is ~15–50% above these sale prices before any Lisbon overhead or margin.

### Gaps
- No fully-loaded employer-side cost per FTE for India/Philippines (only provider sale prices and salaries).
- No SA provider selling to UK practices with published prices.

## Software costs per client

### Takeaway
Xero Ignite now £18/month (from Sept 2026), FreeAgent £19 sole trader / £10 landlord (free with NatWest/RBS/Ulster/Mettle accounts), bridging ~£30/year per NI number, Dext roughly £7.80–£21.50 per client/month depending on volume.

### Cited Findings
- Xero UK from 1 Sept 2026: Ignite £16→£18, Grow £37→£39, Comprehensive £50→£55, Ultimate £65→£70 (ex VAT) — [Marc Andrews](https://marcandrews.com/xero-price-rise-september-2026-uk/); [Finn Accountings](https://www.finnaccountings.com/xero-price-increase-2026)
- FreeAgent £19/month sole trader, £10/month unincorporated landlord (ex VAT); free with NatWest, Mettle, RBS or Ulster Bank business account — [Compare the Cloud](https://www.comparethecloud.net/articles/freeagent-quickbooks-or-sage-for-the-new-30000-making-tax-digital-threshold) (snippet)
- Bridging: VitalTax £30 + VAT per NI number/year (or £198 unlimited); Tax Optimiser ~£30/year — [MTD Compare](https://mtdcompare.co.uk/guides/mtd-bridging-software/) (snippet)
- Dext UK practice pricing: £391/month for 50 clients (£7.82/client), £894.15 for 150, £1,541.10 for 300; ~£21.50/client at 10 clients; quote-based — [ReceiptFlow](https://receiptflow.co/blog/dext-pricing-uk-accounting-practices-2026) (competitor blog; treat with caution); official page [Dext partner pricing](https://dext.com/uk/partner/pricing) not fetched

### Inferences
- Software is a pass-through of roughly £2.50–£40 per client/month depending on stack; in a white-label model the client or practice usually holds the licence, so the back office need not carry it.

### Gaps
- QuickBooks UK 2026 prices and Xero partner discounts not verified.
