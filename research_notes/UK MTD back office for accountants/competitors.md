# Competitive landscape: outsourced / white-label bookkeeping and MTD for ITSA services sold to UK accountancy practices (as of 2026-10-04)

Method note: ~18 tool calls (web search + fetch). Where a fact comes from a *competitor's* listicle (e.g. AcoBloom profiling rivals) rather than the company's own site, it is flagged "(third-party listicle)". Price ranges published by outsourcers on their blogs are often *market* ranges, not their own rate card; that distinction is flagged per line. Nothing below is estimated unless marked ESTIMATE.

## Q1. Who already sells this to UK practices, how big, where, how priced, how positioned?

### Takeaway
The market is crowded and mature: at least eight India-delivered outsourcers each claim 200 to 3,500+ staff and 100 to 500+ UK practice clients, nearly all hold ISO 27001, and nearly all already market "MTD-ready" bookkeeping. Published price points cluster at £8 to £25/hour offshore, roughly £1,200 to £2,800/month per dedicated FTE, and free trials of about 10 hours. A dedicated "MTD for ITSA white-label" niche exists but is thin — one UK-staffed example (Digital Tax Service) publishes no prices.

### Inferences
- Price is not a differentiator: offshore hourly ranges (£8–£25, ads from £9/hr) and FTE ranges (£1,200–£2,800/month) are widely published, and free 10-hour trials are standard (Outbooks, Initor).
- ISO 27001 is table stakes (QX not confirmed on page, but Advancetrack, Corient, Initor, IRIS, Outbooks, AcoBloom all claim it). Cyber Essentials found only for Corient.
- Almost every provider sells generalist capacity (bookkeeping, accounts, tax, payroll) with "MTD-ready" as a label. Few productise the MTD for ITSA *quarterly cycle* (eligibility, sign-up, migration, quarterly updates) as a white-label line; Digital Tax Service and IRIS ("MTD Services") are the clearest cases found.
- Sales emphasis: years in business, ICAEW/ACCA staff, data security ("zero data breaches"), and "save 40–50%+".

## Q2. Are any providers nearshore rather than India or the Philippines, and who sells UK time zone as a differentiator?

### Takeaway
Nearshore supply exists but is small and mostly not focused on UK accountancy practices. Connect Macedonia (North Macedonia staff) explicitly sells "UK timezone" accountants to UK firms; Accace runs a South African office doing bookkeeping for a UK practice. Meanwhile the India players blunt the time-zone argument by advertising UK business hours (IRIS: 9–5).

### Cited Findings
- Connect Macedonia: staff in North Macedonia / "South East Europe", "working in UK timezone", "save 50-75% accounting staff costs", "$0 Mark Up", "No lock-in contracts", "60-day terms", "Monthly Contract", hire "in under 14 days"; staff "trained in VAT, HMRC reporting, UK GAAP/IFRS". It has a pricing table, but the fetch did not return its figures — [Connect Macedonia](https://connectmkd.com/nearshore-accounting-services-uk/)
- Accace Adept South Africa publishes a case study of "full outsourced bookkeeping services for UK accounting practice" via a South African delivery office at "an affordable hourly rate" (rate not stated) — [Accace SA](https://accace.co.za/about-us/case-studies/full-outsourced-bookkeeping-services/)
- Accace operates in Poland, Romania, Ukraine and the UK; Accountor has a Ukrainian outsourcing team using Xero and QuickBooks — these are general business accounting, not white-label for UK practices — [Accace](https://www.accace.com/outsourcing/accounting/), [Accountor Ukraine](https://www.accountor.com/en/ukraine/outsourced-accounting-services-companies-europe-full-gdpr-compliance)
- Polish accountants cost "$18 to $35 per hour" (aggregator, unverified) — [1840 & Co](https://www.1840andco.com/blog/nearshore-vs-offshore-outsourcing)
- IRIS's India team advertises UK business hours, 9 AM–5 PM — [IRIS Outsourcing](https://www.iris.co.uk/products/iris-outsourcing/)
- Outbooks lists an office in Ireland and Malta alongside India (delivery mix not specified) — [Outbooks](https://outbooks.co.uk/)

### Inferences
- A "European team, UK hours" positioning is not crowded among outsourcers that sell *to accountancy practices*. But it's a staffing claim the incumbents can partly match by running UK-hours shifts.
- Nearshore cost (~$18–35/hour for Poland per one aggregator) sits above India offshore (£8–25/hour), so nearshore has to sell on time overlap, language and GDPR comfort, not price.

### Gaps
- No nearshore provider found with a published £ price for UK-practice bookkeeping or MTD work. No Portugal-based or Romania-based provider found that sells white-label work to UK practices specifically.

## Q3. MTD software vendors: done-for-you services, partner or outsourcer programmes, marketplaces

### Takeaway
IRIS is the clearest case of a software vendor that also competes directly: IRIS Outsourcing has 600+ staff in Chennai and sells MTD services to practices. For the other vendors, this round found little: the main overlap is that outsourcers list compatibility with Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, FreeAgent, TaxCalc, and some (ECloud Experts) market Xero partner status.

### Cited Findings
- IRIS Outsourcing / IRIS KPO (Chennai) offers accountancy and bookkeeping outsourcing to UK accountants, including MTD for VAT and Income Tax with "digital onboarding" — [IRIS outsourcing services](https://www.iris.co.uk/accounting/services/outsourcing/), [IRIS Outsourcing product](https://www.iris.co.uk/products/iris-outsourcing/)
- BrightTax (Bright, brightsg.com) markets MTD-ready workflows for MTD for ITSA from April 2026, for sole traders and landlords over £50,000 — software, not done-for-you — [BrightTax](https://brightsg.com/brighttax-cloud-tax-accounts-production-software/)
- ECloud Experts markets itself as a "Xero Gold Champion Partner" selling outsourced Xero bookkeeping — [ECloud Experts](https://ecloud-experts.com/outsource-xero-bookkeeping-service/)

### Inferences
- IRIS bundling software + offshore MTD processing is the most direct vendor-side threat; it could do the same for practices on IRIS that are short on capacity.

### Gaps
- Not researched within budget: Xero App Store / Advisor directory listings of outsourcers; Sage Accountant Partner programme; QuickBooks ProAdvisor; FreeAgent, TaxCalc, Hammock, Landlord Studio, Capium, Clear Books, BTCSoftware partner or done-for-you offers. This needs a follow-up pass.

## Q4. AI-first entrants automating bookkeeping or MTD categorisation

### Takeaway
Capture and categorisation tools (Dext, Hubdoc, bank-feed rules) are mainstream, priced per client per month, and claimed to cut data entry 60–80%. They shrink the hours a people-plus-software service can bill, but they don't replace the review, chasing and filing work. No UK "AI bookkeeper" startup with a published done-for-you MTD price was confirmed in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Dext pricing "$10-$25 per client monthly", "Dext Prepare ... $5-$10/client monthly"; also "starts around £38/month for sole traders"; capture accuracy "95-97%" (aggregator listicle, not Dext's own price page) — [AI tools roundup (search summary)](https://www.aibyrole.co.uk/guides/best-ai-tools-accountants-uk-2026)
- Claim: Dext, Hubdoc and bank feed rules "cut data entry by 60 to 80 percent for most practices" (aggregator) — [search result set incl. Briefcase](https://briefcase.so/blog/best-ai-bookkeeping-software-uk-accountants)
- AccountsOS: AI categorisation "£20/month per company" standard plan, practice discounts — [AccountsOS](https://accounts-os.com/blog/accounting-software-for-uk-practice-firms-2026)
- Several practice-focused AI bookkeeping tools are marketed to UK firms in 2026 (Briefcase, Tofu, bankreconciler.app) — [Briefcase](https://briefcase.so/blog/best-ai-bookkeeping-software-uk-accountants), [Tofu](https://www.gotofu.com/blog/best-bookkeeping-automation-software-uk-accounting-firms)

### Inferences
- If software at £20/company/month automates most categorisation, the value of a human back office shifts to exceptions, client chasing and the quarterly submission workflow. ESTIMATE: a price based on hours per client is at risk; a price per quarterly update is less exposed.

### Gaps
- Fyle and the claimed accuracy of AI categorisation on UK sole-trader/landlord data not verified from primary sources. No UK "Pilot-style" AI-plus-human firm was confirmed.

## Q5. Saturation and consolidation

### Takeaway
There are clear signs of consolidation. PE-backed buyers are acquiring Indian outsourcers (Springline, November 2025). UK roll-ups (Xeinadin, Sumer, Azets) keep buying practices. 44% of the UK top 100 did M&A in 2025. Roll-ups that build their own offshore capacity shrink the addressable market of independent practices.

### Cited Findings
- PE-backed Springline Advisory acquired Ahmedabad outsourcers Smart Accountants and Infinity Globus on 5 Nov 2025 (+191 staff; group >750) — [The Finance Story](https://thefinancestory.com/indian-accounting-kpos-rushing-for-private-equity-investment)
- Same source: Indian outsourcing firms reportedly courting PE, with 2026 tipped as an "acquisition season" (speculative) — same source
- "44% of the UK's top 100 accountancy firms engaged in M&A activity in 2025" (search summary) — [Accountancy Today acquisitions tag](https://www.accountancytoday.co.uk/tag/acquisition/)
- Xeinadin (Exponent-backed) prepared a possible £800m sale — [Accountancy Age](https://accountancyage.com/2025/05/27/xeinadin-prepares-for-potential-800m-sale-to-private-equity/); the sale was abandoned after bids missed a £1bn+ valuation — [Private Equity Wire](https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/exponent-abandons-1bn-sale-of-uk-accountancy-group-xeinadin/)
- Azets continues acquiring UK practices (e.g. Alextra Accountants) — [Outsource Accelerator](https://news.outsourceaccelerator.com/azets-alextra-accountants/)
- Sumer is a Penta Capital-backed roll-up led by ex-KPMG UK COO Warren Mead — [Henrico Dolfing case study](https://www.henricodolfing.ch/en/case-study-37-xeinadin-sumer-and-the-hidden-assumption-behind-accounting-roll-ups/)

### Inferences
- Roll-ups are unlikely buyers of a small external back office if they run captive offshore teams. The likely customers are the independent small practices left outside them.

### Gaps
- Did not confirm whether Xeinadin, Sumer or Azets run captive offshore centres (no source found in this pass). Acquisitions of UK-facing outsourcers specifically (e.g. QX, Outbooks, Corient ownership changes) were not found.
